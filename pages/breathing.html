Box Breathing Exercise

Four equal sides of breath: Inhale 4s, Hold 4s, Exhale 4s, Hold 4s. One complete 16-second cycle resets your autonomic nervous system.

Science of Breath

How Box Breathing Works in the Body

Understanding the physiology behind 16-second respiratory cycles.

🫀

Vagus Nerve Stimulation

Slow rhythmic inhalation followed by extended retention directly activates parasympathetic vagal tone, lowering high heart rates.

⚡

Stress Hormone Reduction

Within just 4 to 8 cycles (2 to 3 minutes), levels of blood cortisol and adrenaline decrease noticeably, restoring composure.

🛡️

High-Performance Focus

Used by tactical forces and elite athletes under pressure to maintain steady motor precision and emotional equilibrium.

💡

Gentle Practice Advice

Always breathe smoothly through the diaphragm without hyperventilating or straining. If you feel lightheaded at any point, pause the exercise and return to your natural effortless breathing pattern.
